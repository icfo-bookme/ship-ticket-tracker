## Getting Started

### Accessing the Application

1. Open your web browser and navigate to the application URL
2. You'll see the public booking form on the home page
3. To access advanced features (viewing all your bookings, payments, refunds), you may need to **Log In** or **Register**

### Creating an Account

If you don't have an account:

1. Click on **Register** link on the login page
2. Enter the following information:
   - **Name**: Your full name
   - **Email**: A valid email address
   - **Password**: Create a strong password (at least 8 characters)
   - **Confirm Password**: Re-enter your password
3. Click **Register** button
4. Check your email for verification link (if required)
5. Once verified, you can log in with your credentials

### Logging In

1. Click **Login** on the home page
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**
3. Click **Login** button
4. You'll be redirected to your **Dashboard**

## Booking a Ticket

### Method 1: Public Booking Form (No Login Required)

This is the quickest way to book a ticket without creating an account.

#### Step 1: Select Shipping Details

1. On the home page, you'll see the booking form
2. Fill in the following fields:

   **Required Information:**
   - **Select Company**: Choose the shipping company
   - **Select Ship/Route**: Choose your departure route
   - **Select Package Tier**: Choose your ticket package:
     - **Standard Package**: Basic ticket
     - **Premium Package**: Premium ticket with additional features
     - **Deluxe Package**: Premium service with extra benefits

#### Step 2: Enter Passenger Information

1. **Primary Passenger Details** (the main traveler):
   - **Name**: Full name of the passenger
   - **Email**: Valid email address
   - **Phone**: Contact phone number
   - **National ID (NID)**: National identification number

2. **Number of Co-Passengers**: Enter if there are additional passengers
   - **Add Co-Passengers**: If you selected multiple passengers, add their details:
     - Name
     - Email
     - Phone
     - National ID

3. **WhatsApp Contact** (Optional):
   - Enter WhatsApp number if you want updates via WhatsApp
   - Helpful for receiving booking confirmation and shipment updates

#### Step 3: Review & Submit

1. Review all information for accuracy
2. Click **Submit** or **Book Now** button
3. A confirmation page will appear with:
   - **Booking Reference Number** (Save this!)
   - **Amount Due**
   - **Payment Instructions**

### Method 2: Logged-In Booking

If you're logged into the system:

1. Go to **New Booking** or **Create Ticket**
2. Follow the same steps as above
3. The system may pre-fill some information from your profile

## Ticket Status Tracking

### Ticket Lifecycle

Every ticket goes through the following stages:

| Status | Meaning | Timeline |
|--------|---------|----------|
| **Pending** | Booking received, awaiting payment | Initial stage |
| **Payment Verified** | Payment confirmed and verified | After payment processing |
| **Ticket Issued** | Ticket has been issued to passenger | After payment verification |
| **Ticket Printed** | Physical ticket has been printed | Before departure |
| **Parcel Created** | Shipment created for delivery | Intermediate stage |
| **Shipped** | Package is with courier for delivery | Final stage |

### Checking Your Ticket Status

#### From Public Booking Page:
1. Enter your **Booking Reference Number** (provided at booking)
2. Enter your **Email** address
3. Click **Check Status**
4. View your current ticket status and details

#### From Your Account (If Logged In):
1. Log in to your account
2. Go to **My Bookings** or **Tickets**
3. View all your bookings and their current status
4. Click on any booking to see detailed information

## Payment Management

### Payment Methods

The system supports the following payment methods:

- **Cash**: Pay when picking up or at designated locations
- **bKash**: Mobile banking payment (Bangladesh)
- **Nagad**: Mobile money payment (Bangladesh)
- **Bank Transfer**: Direct bank deposit

### Making a Payment

#### Initial Payment During Booking:

1. After booking, you'll see the amount due
2. Choose your preferred payment method
3. Follow the payment instructions specific to your method:

   **For bKash (Mobile Banking):**
   - Open bKash app → Send Money
   - Enter the merchant account provided
   - Enter the amount
   - Confirm the payment
   - You'll receive a confirmation code

   **For Nagad (Mobile Money):**
   - Open Nagad app → Send Money/Payment
   - Enter merchant details
   - Enter amount and complete confirmation
   - Wait for payment notification

   **For Bank Transfer:**
   - Use the bank details provided
   - Include your Booking Reference Number as reference
   - Transfer amount and wait for verification

   **For Cash:**
   - Contact the company at provided WhatsApp or phone number
   - Arrange pickup or payment at office
   - Get payment confirmation receipt

#### Paying Outstanding Balance:

If your payment was partial:

1. Log in to your account
2. Go to **My Bookings**
3. Find the booking with **Outstanding Balance**
4. Click **Pay Balance** button
5. Enter the remaining amount
6. Choose payment method and complete

### Payment Receipt

After successful payment:
- You'll receive an email confirmation
- Reference number will be provided
- Payment status will update to "Payment Verified"
- Your ticket will be issued

## Refunds

### Understanding Refunds

- **Full Refund**: You get back the entire amount you paid
- **Partial Refund**: You get back a portion of the amount (if applicable)
- **Refund Eligibility**: Depends on company policy and ticket status

### Requesting a Refund

#### Through Your Account:

1. Log in to your account
2. Go to **My Bookings**
3. Find the ticket you want to refund
4. Click **Request Refund** button
5. Choose refund type:
   - **Full Refund**: Return entire amount
   - **Partial Refund**: Enter the amount you want back
6. Provide reason for refund (optional)
7. Submit request

#### Contact Support:

1. If the refund button is not available, contact support
2. Provide your **Booking Reference Number**
3. Specify the reason for refund
4. Wait for refund approval

### Refund Status

After requesting a refund:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Refund Requested** | Your refund request has been received |
| **Refund Approved** | Your refund has been approved |
| **Refund Processed** | Money has been sent back to your account |

**Processing Time:**
- The refund will be processed within 3-7 business days
- You'll receive a notification and email confirmation

## Printing & Documents

### Downloading Your Ticket

Once your ticket is issued and printed:

1. Log in to your account
2. Go to **My Bookings**
3. Find the ticket with status **Ticket Printed**
4. Click **Download Ticket** or **Print Ticket**
5. Select format:
   - **PDF**: Download as PDF file
   - **Print**: Print directly from your browser

### Accessing Your Ticket

You can access your ticket through:

1. **Email**: Check your email for ticket attachment
2. **Account**: Log in and download from My Bookings
3. **WhatsApp**: If WhatsApp contact provided, receive via WhatsApp

### Printing Tips

- **Color Printing**: Recommended for barcode scanning
- **Paper Size**: Use A4 or standard letter size
- **Quality**: Print in high quality to ensure barcode is scannable
- **Keep Copies**: Keep both digital and printed copies

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Booking & Tickets

**Q: How long is my Booking Reference Number valid?**
A: Your booking is valid until the ticket status reaches "Parcel Created". After that, it's in shipment.

**Q: Can I change my booking after submitting?**
A: Contact support with your Booking Reference Number for modifications. Some changes may not be possible.

**Q: What if I book multiple tickets?**
A: Each passenger or co-passenger booking gets its own Booking Reference Number and ticket.

**Q: Is a login required to book?**
A: No, the public booking form doesn't require login. However, creating an account helps you track all bookings.

### Payments

**Q: What happens if I don't pay?**
A: Your ticket status remains "Pending". You have a grace period to make payment (check company policy).

**Q: Can I use multiple payment methods?**
A: Yes, for partial payments, you can use different methods for each payment.

**Q: Is my payment information secure?**
A: Yes, all payments are processed through secure channels with encryption.

**Q: Do I get a receipt for my payment?**
A: Yes, you'll receive an email receipt and can download it from your account.

### Refunds

**Q: How long does a refund take?**
A: Refunds are typically processed within 3-7 business days after approval.

**Q: Can I get a refund after the ticket is printed?**
A: Some companies may allow refunds even after printing. Contact support for details.

**Q: What if my refund doesn't arrive?**
A: Check your bank account after 7 business days. Contact support with your Refund Reference Number if it's not received.

### Tickets & Documents

**Q: Can I print my ticket multiple times?**
A: Yes, you can download and print your ticket as many times as needed.

**Q: What if I lost my ticket?**
A: You can redownload it from your account. Your Booking Reference Number serves as backup.

**Q: Can I share my ticket with someone else?**
A: Tickets are issued in the passenger's name. Check with the company about transferring tickets.

**Q: What barcode information does my ticket contain?**
A: The barcode contains your Booking Reference Number and ticket details.

## Troubleshooting

### Booking Issues

**Problem: "Duplicate Booking Detected" message**
- **Solution**: The system detected a duplicate booking. Check if you already booked with the same details. Contact support if this is an error.

**Problem: Can't select a ship/route**
- **Solution**: The route may be unavailable. Try selecting a different date or company.

**Problem: Form won't submit**
- **Solution**: Check that all required fields are filled correctly. Look for red error messages and correct them.

### Payment Issues

**Problem: Payment took money but status still shows "Pending"**
- **Solution**: Wait 5-10 minutes for verification. Payment verification can take time. If it's still pending after 30 minutes, contact support with your payment reference.

**Problem: Payment failed but money was deducted**
- **Solution**: Check your bank account and payment app. Contact your bank to verify. Provide proof of deduction to support for refund.

**Problem: Can't find payment options**
- **Solution**: Ensure you've completed the booking first. Payment options appear on the confirmation page.

### Ticket & Printing Issues

**Problem: Can't download my ticket**
- **Solution**:
  - Check if ticket status is "Ticket Printed"
  - Try using a different browser
  - Clear your browser cache and try again
  - Contact support with your Booking Reference Number

**Problem: Barcode won't scan**
- **Solution**:
  - Print in high quality (not draft mode)
  - Use color printer if possible
  - Ensure barcode is fully printed without damage
  - Try scanning with different barcode reader

**Problem: Ticket information is incorrect**
- **Solution**: Contact support immediately with your Booking Reference Number and details of the error.

### Account Issues

**Problem: Can't log in to my account**
- **Solution**:
  - Check that you're using correct email and password
  - Try "Forgot Password" to reset
  - Clear browser cookies and try again
  - Contact support for account recovery

**Problem: Email not received for verification**
- **Solution**:
  - Check spam/junk folder
  - Wait a few minutes and try again
  - Contact support to resend verification email

**Problem: Password reset not working**
- **Solution**:
  - Check spam folder for password reset email
  - Try resetting again
  - Contact support for manual password reset

### General Issues

**Problem: Application is slow or unresponsive**
- **Solution**:
  - Refresh the page (F5)
  - Clear browser cache
  - Try a different browser
  - Check your internet connection

**Problem: Error message appears but I don't understand it**
- **Solution**:
  - Note the exact error message
  - Take a screenshot
  - Contact support with this information
